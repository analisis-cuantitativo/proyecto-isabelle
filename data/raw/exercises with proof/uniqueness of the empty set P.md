Proof: suppose that the sets $Y$ and $Z$ satisfy the condition of emptiness. this means that for all $x$, $\neg(x \in Y)$ and $\neg(x \in Z)$ are always true statements. From here, it follows that $x \in Y$ and $x \in Z$ are always false statements. Therefore, $x \in Y$ holds if and only if $x \in Z$ holds, which implies that $Y=Z$
