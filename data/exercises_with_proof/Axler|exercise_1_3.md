Prove that $-(-v) = v$ for every $v \in V$.

Proof:
\begin{proof}

    By definition, we have

$$

(-v)+(-(-v))=0 \quad \text { and } \quad v+(-v)=0 .

$$

This implies both $v$ and $-(-v)$ are additive inverses of $-v$, by the uniqueness of additive inverse, it follows that $-(-v)=v$.

\end{proof}
